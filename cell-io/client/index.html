Controls

  • Mouse: steer (distance = speed)
  • Space: dash burst
  • Q: toxin cloud (needs a Toxin Gland)
  • R: venom surge (needs a linked Venom Sac)
  • E: open evolution
  • M: mute
  • F3: network stats

How to play

  • Eat nutrients for biomass (size) and XP (level).
  • Each level, pick an organelle and attach it where you want.
  • Placement matters: front spikes ram, rear spikes punish chasers, armor only protects the side it covers.
  • Glowing crystals near acid pools give lots of XP.

Try a Hunter (front spikes, flagellum, venom), a Tank (armor, regen, mitochondria), a Speedster (cilia clusters, flagellum) or a Toxic Defender (toxin, armor, regen).
